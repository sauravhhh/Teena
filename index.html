Chapter Four

A letter for you, Teena

Before the last page, there is something that deserved to be said properly, in writing.

♥

Dear Teena,

There is something I have wanted to say to you properly, so I am writing it here, where it will not get lost.

You are pretty, Teena. Not just in photos, though you look lovely in every single one of them. You are pretty in the way you smile, the way it reaches your eyes before you even realise it. You are pretty in the way you talk, in your laugh, and in the small kindnesses you do without thinking twice.

Most of all, you make ordinary days feel special. A normal afternoon, a short conversation, a simple hello from you, and somehow the whole day feels lighter and warmer. That is a rare thing, and I do not think you know how much it means.

I just wanted you to know that someone notices. Someone is grateful that you are exactly the way you are.

With all my heart,

Saurabh
